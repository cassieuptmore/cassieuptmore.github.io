The Thesis

Win the room — and the category writes itself

Kodex is solving a problem every enterprise legal, privacy, and Trust & Safety team has — and most are still handling it by email and spreadsheet. The field marketing opportunity is unusually asymmetric: the ICP is a defined, knowable community (Trust & Safety, Legal, Privacy, Compliance leaders at enterprise tech), the buying signal is clear (any company fielding government data requests), and Kodex already has the logos that validate every sale.

The field motion should not feel like enterprise software marketing. It should feel like Kodex convenes the smartest room in the T&S and legal compliance community — where the founder's FBI background makes every conversation credible in a way no competitor can fake, and where every dinner, roundtable, and conference presence tells the same story: the people who already trust Kodex are the companies everyone else wants to be.

"Kodex is already in the room. The operating system makes sure we own it — and that every legal and T&S leader who isn't yet a customer feels the gap."

The Three-Tier Model

Tiering is the highest-leverage decision a small team makes

Go deep on the T&S flagship moments. Systematize the industry calendar. Run a relentless executive dinner cadence that makes Kodex feel omnipresent in this community.

Tier 1 · Flagship

Category-defining presence

TrustCon, Trust & Safety Summit — the two events every T&S leader attends. Kodex should own the room: speaking slot, dinner, curated executive meeting program with target accounts.

Cadence: 2–3 flagship/yr
Investment: $40K–$150K
KPI: Pipeline influenced, exec meetings
Tier 2 · Industry

Vertical footprint

Marketplace Risk, TSPA EMEA, CLOC (legal ops), ILTACON (legal tech), RSA/Black Hat (compliance angle), Compliance Week. Systematic presence at 10–16 events/yr with lead capture and follow-up SLAs.

Cadence: 10–16/yr
Investment: $8K–$30K each
KPI: Cost/lead, accounts engaged
Tier 3 · Intimate

The omnipresence engine

Executive dinners and roundtables for T&S and Legal leaders — 8–12 people, founder-hosted, peer-to-peer format. This is the highest-ROI motion given the defined, small ICP. Run 30–40/yr at scale.

Cadence: 2–4/month
Investment: $3K–$10K each
KPI: 8–12x ROI, meetings booked

AI-Enabled Operations

One person operates like a team of five

Not chatbot workflows — governed systems where AI handles volume and a human owns judgment. Explicitly what the role requires.

Scout

ICP Research & Account Intel

AI maps every VP of T&S, Legal, and Privacy at Tier 1 target accounts. Identifies who's attended TrustCon, who's spoken on compliance panels, who's a warm connection. The ICP is finite — own the full list.

Scribe

Personalized Outreach at Scale

AI drafts event invites, landing page copy, and post-event follow-up sequences tuned to persona and deal stage. Human curates for voice and relationship context. Speed without sacrifice.

Analyst

Attribution & Pipeline Reporting

AI surfaces cost-per-meeting, influenced pipeline by event, and account engagement scores from HubSpot/Salesforce. Quarterly rebalance is data-driven — not a budget argument.

Concierge

Dinner & Event Logistics

AI handles RSVP tracking, dietary confirmations, reminder sequences, and personalized pre-event briefing docs for sales. The founder walks into every dinner knowing every guest.

Playbook

Runbooks & Institutional Memory

Every workflow documented in AI-assisted runbooks that anyone can run. Nothing lives in one person's head. The system scales without the team scaling first.

Governance

Human-in-the-Loop on What Matters

AI runs autonomously on internal ops (research, logistics, reporting). All customer-facing outputs — invites, follow-up, founder briefings — require human review. One error there invalidates brand trust.
