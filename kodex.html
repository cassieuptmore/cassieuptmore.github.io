The AI Operating Model

How a lean team produces category-leader event & field assets

Not by chatting with a chatbot — by turning repeatable tasks into shared, governed workflows. For a security/trust company, the governance layer isn't optional; it's on-brand.

The Marketing Brain

A versioned repo of prompts & "skills" encoding Kodex SOPs, brand, messaging & the threat-intel framing. Invoke a "roundtable" or "threat-intel briefing" skill → on-brand output in seconds.

Zero-to-One workflows

Automate the first 80%. From one Threat-Intel theme: research target accounts/agencies, draft the briefing deck, the invite sequence, and the follow-up — in minutes.

Mass personalization

HeyGen-style AI video where Matt (ex-FBI founder) or a threat analyst invites each registrant by name. An "unfair" high-touch move competitors can't match.

Sales-enablement "brains"

An approved library so revenue can generate bespoke, on-brand follow-ups and briefs off every event in real time — closing the marketing-to-sales gap.

Content & SEO engines

Programmatic SEO for "law-enforcement data request" by jurisdiction/vertical; quarterly refresh keeps pages ~3× more likely cited by AI search.

Thought partner — CRIT

Not an oracle. CRIT (Context, Role, Interview, Task) makes AI interview you one question at a time to surface plan gaps before a dollar is spent.

Governance — a risk-level operating model (and Kodex's home turf). Low-risk internal agents (monitor, summarize, report — "Sprint") run autonomously. High-risk, customer- or agency-facing outputs (personalized outreach, anything touching sensitive data) always require human-in-the-loop review. For a trust company, this discipline doubles as a proof point.

KPIs & Measurement

Commercial major, marketing minor — plus category authority

Measure on the numbers a board respects — pipeline and pipeline velocity — and, because Kodex is creating a category, authority metrics too. Not vanity counts.

Commercial (the headline)

  • Sourced + influenced pipeline · pipeline velocity
  • Meetings booked with target accounts & agencies
  • Win-rate lift on event-touched deals · cost per opp
  • 48-hour follow-up SLA compliance

Category authority (the moat)

  • Share of voice in T&S / compliance / privacy
  • Threat-Intel report reach & citations
  • Government/agency relationships built
  • Exec advisory council & reference depth
De-emphasize vanity metrics — booth scans, impressions, raw registrations. In a trust category, the quality of the room beats the size of the list.
The dashboard: one live view refreshed by the Analyst agent — pipeline by tier, velocity, account/agency coverage, and a portfolio scorecard so the quarterly rebalance is data-driven, not political.
