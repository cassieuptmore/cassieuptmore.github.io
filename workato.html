The AI Operating Model

Run the field on Workato's own agents — and turn the marketing org into a live demo

Not by chatting with a chatbot — by turning repeatable tasks into shared, governed workflows. For Workato uniquely, the AI operating model is also product marketing: every agent the team uses is a story sales can tell.

The Marketing Brain

A versioned repo of prompts & "skills" encoding Workato brand, messaging & the agentic narrative. Invoke a "WOW session" or "CIO dinner" skill → on-brand output in seconds.

Zero-to-One workflows

Automate the first 80%. From one launch theme: research accounts, draft the abstract, the invite sequence, and the follow-up — built as Workato recipes & agents.

Mass personalization

HeyGen-style video where a Workato leader invites each target CIO by name. "Unfair" high-touch at scale that nimble rivals can't match.

Sales-enablement "brains"

An approved library so AEs generate bespoke, on-brand follow-ups & agent demos off every event in real time — closing the marketing-to-sales gap.

Content & SEO engines

Programmatic SEO for "enterprise agentic AI / MCP" by function & industry; quarterly refresh keeps pages ~3× more likely cited by AI search.

Thought partner — CRIT

Not an oracle. CRIT (Context, Role, Interview, Task) makes AI interview you one question at a time to find plan gaps before a dollar is spent.

The Workato-only unlock — "drink your own champagne." Build the entire pod on Agent Studio + Enterprise MCP. The field team becomes customer-zero: a credible, on-stage case study ("here's how our own marketing runs on Workato agents") that no competitor can copy — and a governance showcase (human-in-the-loop on everything customer-facing) that proves the enterprise-trust promise.

KPIs & Measurement

Commercial major, marketing minor — plus narrative leadership

Measure on the numbers a board respects — pipeline and pipeline velocity — and, because this is a category-leadership fight, on narrative-share in agentic AI. Not vanity counts.

Commercial (the headline)

  • Sourced + influenced pipeline · pipeline velocity
  • Meetings booked with named enterprise accounts
  • Win-rate lift on event-touched deals · cost per opp
  • Expansion / multi-function land within accounts

Narrative leadership (the moat)

  • Share of voice in "enterprise agentic AI / MCP"
  • Analyst standing (Gartner/Forrester) momentum
  • WOW reach, attendance & earned media
  • CIO/CAIO relationships & CAB depth
De-emphasize vanity metrics — booth scans, impressions, raw registrations. For the leader, owning the agentic-AI conversation and the enterprise pipeline beats counting badges.
The dashboard: one live view refreshed by the Analyst agent — pipeline by tier & function, velocity, account coverage, narrative-share trend, and a portfolio scorecard so the quarterly rebalance is data-driven, not political.
