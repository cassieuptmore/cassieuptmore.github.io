The AI Operating Model

Run the field on Scribe — every runbook a public Scribe, the marketing org a live demo

Not by chatting with a chatbot — by turning repeatable tasks into shared, governed workflows. For Scribe uniquely, the operating model is also the product story: every Scribe the team publishes is proof the product works.

The Marketing Brain

A versioned repo of prompts, "skills" & Scribes encoding Scribe's brand & the AI-workflow narrative. Invoke a "champion meetup" or "summit session" skill → on-brand output in seconds.

Zero-to-One workflows

Automate the first 80%. From one event topic: research accounts, draft the invite sequence & follow-up, and publish the run-of-show as a Scribe — in minutes.

Mass personalization

HeyGen-style video where a Scribe leader invites each champion/exec by name. An "unfair" high-touch move at PLG scale.

Sales-enablement "brains"

An approved library so AEs generate bespoke, on-brand follow-ups off every event in real time — turning PLG signups into enterprise conversations.

Content & SEO engines

Programmatic SEO + community Scribes for "how to [do X]" by tool & function; quarterly refresh keeps pages ~3× more likely cited by AI search.

Thought partner — CRIT

Not an oracle. CRIT (Context, Role, Interview, Task) makes AI interview you one question at a time to find plan gaps before a dollar is spent.

The Scribe-only unlock — "show your work." Build the pod on Scribe and its AI workflow-mapping; publish the field team's runbooks as public Scribes and let the workflow-mapping measure the marketing process itself. The field org becomes a customer-zero showcase ("here's how we run on Scribe") that no competitor can copy — and governance (human-in-the-loop on everything customer-facing) keeps the brand safe.

KPIs & Measurement

Commercial major, marketing minor — plus PLG & community signal

Measure on the numbers a board respects — pipeline and pipeline velocity — alongside the PLG & community signals that make Scribe's model special. Not vanity counts.

Commercial (the headline)

  • Sourced + influenced pipeline · pipeline velocity
  • Champion-to-enterprise conversion (PLG → sales)
  • Win-rate lift on event-touched deals · cost per opp
  • Expansion / seats within accounts

PLG & community (the flywheel)

  • Event-driven signups & activated users
  • Active champions & user-generated Scribes
  • Share of voice in "process docs / AI at work"
  • Summit reach, NPS & CAB depth
De-emphasize vanity metrics — booth scans, impressions, raw registrations. For a PLG company, activated users and champion-to-enterprise conversion beat counting badges.
The dashboard: one live view refreshed by the Analyst agent — pipeline by tier, signups & activation, champion conversion, narrative-share, and a portfolio scorecard so the quarterly rebalance is data-driven, not political.
