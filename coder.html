The AI Operating Model

Run the field's AI agents on Coder — the most credible demo a security company can give

Not by chatting with a chatbot — by turning repeatable tasks into shared, governed workflows. For Coder uniquely, the operating model is the product story: a "run AI agents securely" company that runs its own marketing agents securely on Coder is unanswerable proof.

The Marketing Brain

A versioned repo of prompts & "skills" (as code) encoding Coder's brand & the governance narrative. Invoke a "KubeCon booth" or "CISO dinner" skill → on-brand output in seconds.

Zero-to-One workflows

Automate the first 80%. From one launch theme: research accounts, draft the technical abstract, invite sequence & follow-up — run as governed agents on Coder.

Mass personalization

HeyGen-style video where a Coder engineer invites each target CISO/platform lead by name. "Unfair" high-touch at scale.

Sales-enablement "brains"

An approved library so AEs & SEs generate bespoke, on-brand follow-ups & security briefs off every event in real time.

Content & SEO engines

Programmatic SEO & technical content for "self-hosted AI coding agents / secure dev environments"; quarterly refresh → ~3× more likely cited by AI search.

Thought partner — CRIT

Not an oracle. CRIT (Context, Role, Interview, Task) makes AI interview you one question at a time to find plan gaps before a dollar is spent.

The Coder-only unlock — "run it on us." Build the entire pod as governed agents on Coder's own workspaces. The field org becomes the flagship customer-zero proof ("our marketing AI agents run securely on Coder — here's the audit trail") that no cloud rival can match — and human-in-the-loop on everything customer-facing is itself the governance story Coder sells.

KPIs & Measurement

Commercial major, marketing minor — plus developer & narrative signal

Measure on the numbers a board respects — pipeline and pipeline velocity — alongside the developer & narrative signals that decide whether the bottoms-up engine and the category fight are being won. Not vanity counts.

Commercial (the headline)

  • Sourced + influenced pipeline · pipeline velocity
  • Meetings booked with named enterprise / regulated accounts
  • Win-rate lift on event-touched deals · cost per opp
  • Expansion / seats within accounts

Developer & narrative (the moat)

  • OSS signal (stars, contributors), dev signups & activation
  • Share of voice in "secure / self-hosted AI development"
  • KubeCon/PlatformCon presence & earned media
  • CISO/CTO relationships & CAB depth
De-emphasize vanity metrics — booth scans, impressions, raw registrations. For a developer-infra company, activated developers and security-approved enterprise pipeline beat counting badges.
The dashboard: one live view refreshed by the Analyst agent — pipeline by tier, dev signups & activation, narrative-share, account coverage, and a portfolio scorecard so the quarterly rebalance is data-driven.
