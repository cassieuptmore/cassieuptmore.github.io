The Thesis

Skillable's product is hands-on labs — so the field strategy is simple: make every event a live lab

In an AI era where skills beat credentials and buyers want proof, Skillable owns the validated, hands-on way to build, prove, and sell a skill. The most powerful field move is also the most obvious: don't pitch the product — let people prove themselves on it. A system + AI gives a PE-disciplined team 10x–100x leverage, so it can run an experience-led program at scale without growing spend in lockstep. The system, not the headcount, produces quality at volume.

Current State

The opportunity is to make events demonstrate the product by having attendees use it.

  • A multi-use-case platform — skilling & validation, sales-enablement labs, certification, customer support.

The AI Operating Model

AI generates the labs, the content, and the follow-up — humans run the room

Not by chatting with a chatbot — by turning repeatable tasks into shared, governed workflows. For Skillable, AI is also a product accelerant: it can spin up the live-lab challenges that make every event experiential, fast.

The Marketing Brain

A versioned repo of prompts, "skills" & lab templates encoding Skillable's brand & the "prove it" narrative. Invoke a "live-lab booth" or "CLO roundtable" skill → on-brand output in seconds.

Zero-to-One workflows

Automate the first 80%. From one event theme: research accounts, generate the lab challenge, draft the invite & follow-up sequence — in minutes.

Mass personalization

HeyGen-style video where a Skillable leader invites each CLO/enablement lead by name to a hands-on challenge. "Unfair" high-touch at scale.

Sales-enablement "brains"

An approved library so AEs turn a prospect's lab score into a bespoke, on-brand follow-up in real time — hands-on result → conversation.

Content & SEO engines

Programmatic SEO for "hands-on [skill] labs / validate [X] skills" by domain; quarterly refresh keeps pages ~3× more likely cited by AI search.

Thought partner — CRIT

Not an oracle. CRIT (Context, Role, Interview, Task) makes AI interview you one question at a time to find plan gaps before a dollar is spent.

The Skillable-only unlock — "experience it to believe it." Use Skillable to run the field itself: onboard the field team in Skillable labs, power every booth and workshop with a live lab, and let the platform's scoring become the marketing metric (hands-on completion → pipeline). The product proves itself every time someone touches it — with human-in-the-loop on everything customer-facing.

KPIs & Measurement

Commercial major, marketing minor — plus the experiential proof

Measure on the numbers a PE board respects — pipeline and pipeline velocity — alongside the experiential signals that prove the live-lab strategy works. Not vanity counts.

Commercial (the headline)

  • Sourced + influenced pipeline · pipeline velocity
  • Hands-on-to-pipeline conversion (lab → opp)
  • Win-rate lift on event-touched deals · cost per opp
  • Expansion across both buyer worlds

Experiential (the proof)

  • Lab launches & completions at events
  • Active lab authors & community labs created
  • Share of voice in "skills validation / experiential learning"
  • Summit reach, NPS & CAB depth
De-emphasize vanity metrics — booth scans, impressions, raw registrations. For Skillable, a lab someone actually completed beats a badge they scanned — and it converts far better.
The dashboard: one live view refreshed by the Analyst agent — pipeline by tier & buyer world, lab launches, hands-on conversion, narrative-share, and a portfolio scorecard so the quarterly rebalance is data-driven.
