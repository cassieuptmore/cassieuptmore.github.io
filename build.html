Case 01Pipeline Generation

Zero-to-One: $12M Influenced Pipeline in 24 Months

The Challenge

A Series C logistics SaaS company. No event program, no vendors, no playbook, no historical data. Sales asking events to deliver pipeline immediately. Marketing budget: $1M for the year.

The Approach

Built a tiered portfolio so budget went to the highest-intent rooms first. Stood up the vendor ecosystem from zero. Integrated 6Sense intent signals with event registration data so every event tied to a target account list. Designed a custom GPT trained on our ICP, messaging pillars, and brand voice — the team used it for pre-event briefs, on-stand talking points, and follow-up. Built a post-event playbook and ran it after every program.

The Outcome

$12M influenced pipeline on a $1M budget in 24 months — 12× ROI. $4M sourced pipeline. 20+ programs/year running on a documented playbook a successor could run without me.

What it shows. I build event programs as revenue infrastructure, not a calendar.
Case 02Portfolio Architecture

The Tier 1 / 2 / 3 Framework

The Challenge

Same SaaS company. With 60+ industry events on the calendar and a $1M budget, "go to all of them" wasn't an option. Sales wanted everything. Finance wanted nothing.

The Approach

Built a three-tier framework, ran every event through it, and reviewed the portfolio quarterly with sales leadership. Tier 1 — Flagship Bets (4–6/yr, $80–150K each, 25+ meetings). Tier 2 — Targeted Plays (8–12/yr, $20–40K each, 10–15 meetings). Tier 3 — Field & Intimate (12+/yr, $5–15K each, deepen 10–20 named accounts). AI handled first-pass landscape audits and account-density scoring; I made the final call with sales VPs.

The Outcome

Cut the calendar from 40+ events to 22. Pipeline efficiency: +34% pipeline-per-dollar YoY. Quarterly portfolio reviews became standard — events were the only marketing channel sales asked for more of.

What it shows. Director-level event strategy isn't about doing more events. It's about making the portfolio defensible.
Case 03Intent-Driven Field

Intent-Driven Field Marketing Engine

The Challenge

A global SaaS company's NA field team running monthly city dinners that filled seats but didn't move pipeline. Right format, wrong audience.

The Approach

Rebuilt the field engine around intent, not geography. Used 6Sense to surface in-market accounts city-by-city. Pre-event: a custom GPT trained on our ICP and messaging generated per-account executive briefs — title-level talking points, recent funding/M&A signals, conversation hooks. Field reps walked into every dinner with five minutes of relevant context per guest. Replaced "open invite" with intent-qualified guest lists. Built a 14-day AI-personalized, human-reviewed follow-up sequence mapped to the topics each guest engaged with.

The Outcome

3× pipeline per field event within two quarters. 47% of attended accounts advanced to next sales stage within 60 days (vs. 18% baseline). Sales reallocated budget to field events from other channels — first time in three years.

What it shows. Intent data + AI personalization + intimate format = the highest-ROI field play I run.
Case 04Team & Global Ops

Building & Scaling a Global Event Team

The Challenge

The Approach

The Outcome

Scaled to 40+ programs/year across 3 regions without proportional headcount growth. ~40 hrs/month saved on brand review alone. Promoted two of the three regional managers within 18 months.

What it shows. I don't just run programs. I build the team and the system that runs them when I'm not in the room.
Case 05Audience-Mapped Flagship

The Audience-Mapped Flagship Journey

The Challenge

A global SaaS company's annual user conference — 4,500 attendees, three days. Last year's NPS was solid; pipeline impact was murky. CMO wanted one event to drive all GTM goals: pipeline, renewals, advocacy, analyst influence, partner pipeline.

The Approach

Rebuilt the conference as five parallel journeys sharing infrastructure. Mapped every registrant to one of six personas (Net-New, Active Opps, Customers, Champions, Partners, Analysts). Each got a designed journey — pre-event comms, on-site experience, follow-up — owned by the right internal function (sales / CS / partnerships / comms / product marketing). A custom GPT auto-generated AE prep briefs for every active-opportunity attendee using CRM context + session signals.

The Outcome

$28M influenced pipeline in 90 days post-event (vs. $11M prior year, same content). NPS held at 72. 94% of attendees had a journey-defined "moment" they remembered (up from 41%). The journey map became the planning artifact for every subsequent flagship.

What it shows. I architect flagship events as multi-audience GTM machines. The customer journey is the operating model.
